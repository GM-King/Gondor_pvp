# Bundled libraries

HereBeDragons is by Hendrik Leppkes (Nevcairiel), distributed under the BSD license declared in its addon metadata. Source: https://github.com/Nevcairiel/HereBeDragons

The core and pins copies come from PetMaxxing's Forever-compatible bundle, with private Gondor PvP library names and a private pin template to avoid collisions. The map cursor exclusion is applied by Gondor PvP's map adapter.

LibStub and CallbackHandler-1.0 are bundled from Questie's libraries. Their source notices remain intact; the accompanying Ace license is in `Ace-LICENSE.txt`.
